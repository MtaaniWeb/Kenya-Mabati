GAUGE GUIDE

Understanding Mabati Gauges

Gauge refers to the thickness of the roofing sheet. Remember: a lower gauge number generally means a thicker sheet.

Gauge 30

A lighter and more economical option suitable for projects where budget is an important consideration.

Gauge 28

A thicker option offering increased strength and is commonly selected for many roofing projects.

Gauge 26

A thicker roofing option that may be available for selected products. Confirm availability and pricing with our sales team.

Need help choosing? Our team can help you compare the available options for your project.
